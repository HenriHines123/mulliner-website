Guide
How to read this dashboard
01
Percentage changes
Cumulative price movement over that period. Positive = material costs more today = direct risk to construction budgets and feasibility assumptions.
02
Critical risk (≥15%)
Materials up 15%+ over 12 months. High likelihood of causing overruns on fixed-price contracts negotiated 12+ months ago.
03
High risk (8–15%)
Enough to erode builder margins and push lightly contingenced budgets into overrun, especially on fixed-price contracts.
04
RLV impact
Shows how material cost escalation reduces residual land value (RLV) per development type. RLV = GDV minus all costs.
05
Live refresh
Triggers AI web search for current market prices from RLB, ABS, LME, NYMEX, AIP and industry publications.

Data sources
Australian and global market sources searched on each live refresh
Primary — build costs
RLB Riders Digest Australia 2026
Data extracted: Base build cost ranges ($/m² GFA) by development type, Q4 2025. Used for all construction cost ranges on development cards. View source →
Primary — location factors
Rawlinsons Australian Construction Handbook 2025
Data extracted: State and regional location cost factors (NSW, VIC, QLD, SA, WA — metro and regional). Applied to all dev type cost ranges by selected location.
Primary — feasibility ratios
Master Builders Australia
Data extracted: CC as % of GDV benchmarks; material cost as % of CC; construction cost escalation indices. Source: MBA National Construction Pipeline Report & Cost Survey 2025.
Primary — RLV & GDV ratios
Reserve Bank of Australia
Data extracted: Residential property price indices; housing finance statistics; construction-to-value ratios by dwelling type. Source: RBA Housing Finance Statistics & Financial Stability Review.
05 — market data
Property Council of Australia
Data extracted: GDV and RLV ratios for commercial, industrial and retail types; office, retail and industrial market reports.
06 — steel pricing
Australian Steel Institute (AISC)
Data extracted: Domestic structural steel mill pricing (rebar, sections, mesh, roofing); import price benchmarks; tariff impact analysis.
07 — base metals
London Metal Exchange (LME)
Data extracted: Spot and 3-month forward prices for copper, aluminium and zinc. Drives electrical cable, wire/pipe and facade pricing.
08 — crude oil
CME Group / NYMEX
Data extracted: WTI crude oil spot and futures. Primary upstream driver for diesel, bitumen/asphalt and PVC resin pricing.
09 — fuel pricing
Australian Institute of Petroleum
Data extracted: Weekly wholesale and retail diesel prices by state. Direct input for diesel tracker and construction cost escalation via plant and haulage.
10 — construction indices
ABS — Producer Price Index
Data extracted: PPI for construction materials including concrete, timber, steel, plasterboard and insulation. Source: ABS Cat. 6427.0.
11 — residential market
HIA — Housing Industry Association
Data extracted: Residential construction cost escalation; dwelling commencements; townhouse and low-rise apartment cost benchmarks.
12 — FX & commodity
Reserve Bank of Australia — FX
Data extracted: AUD/USD daily exchange rate. Applied to all USD-denominated commodity prices (LME metals, NYMEX crude) to derive AUD construction input costs.
